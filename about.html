Our Story

How it began

GACEB Scholar was founded in 2025 by Sr. Gauge and Sr. Calliper.

The idea was born from a deep desire to raise academic standards and support students with meaningful resources. We were motivated and encouraged by many people, including the valuable guidance of Hajji Muhammed Kamulegeya.

What started as a vision has grown into a platform that serves students across O-Level and A-Level with past papers, practice tools, and a culture of excellence.
