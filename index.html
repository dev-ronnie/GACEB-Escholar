About GACEB Scholar

Owned by Sr. Gauge & Sr. Calliper

GACEB Scholar was founded in 2025 by Sr. Gauge and Sr. Calliper.

The idea was inspired by the support and motivation of many people, including Hajji Muhammed Kamulegeya. What began as a desire to raise academic standards has grown into a professional platform that supports students with quality resources, structured practice and a culture of excellence.

We recognise outstanding teachers through the Gauge Caliper Examination Board and remain committed to helping every student learn with purpose.
